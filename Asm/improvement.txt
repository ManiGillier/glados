⚙️ Optional improvements to your BNF

If you want to make it even more robust:

Whitespace:
Allow optional whitespace between tokens (e.g., <ws> ::= " " | "\t" | <ws> <ws>).
Then use <ws> between grammar elements for flexibility.

Comments:
Add a comment rule like:
<comment> ::= ";" <any_char_sequence>
<line> ::= <comment> | <label> ":" | <instruction> | <instruction> <comment>


Line termination:
If lines are separated by newlines, you can model that with:
<program> ::= <line> "\n" | <line> "\n" <program>
